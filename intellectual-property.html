2. The traditional game of Ronda

Ronda is a traditional card game played across the Maghreb with the Spanish-suited deck. The rules of the traditional game are part of the public domain, and CONPLY claims no ownership over them or over the traditional Spanish-suited card designs in general.

What CONPLY owns is its own implementation: the way the rules are programmed, the original card and table artwork drawn for the App, the game modes (1 vs 1, 2 vs 2, 1 vs 2), the scoring presentation, the online systems and everything listed in section 1.
